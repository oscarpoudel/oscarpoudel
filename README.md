## About me

I am a **Ph.D. candidate in Civil Engineering** at the **New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT)** and a graduate researcher in the **Smart Construction and Intelligent Infrastructure Systems (SCIIS) Lab**. I am also pursuing an **M.S. in Artificial Intelligence** at NJIT.

My work sits at the intersection of **civil engineering, AI, robotics, and intelligent sensing**. I develop and evaluate multimodal systems that connect computer vision, language models, embedded sensing, and autonomous robots with practical engineering workflows.

I am particularly interested in turning research into usable tools, from **on-device crack inspection** and **LLM-based structural analysis** to **human-robot interaction** and **digital infrastructure**.

## Featured projects

| Project | What it does |
